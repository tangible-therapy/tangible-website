Danielle works with teens and adults, specializing in helping people untangle codependency, heal from domestic violence, and move through anxiety, divorce, and low self-esteem. She also has a deep respect for the role faith and spirituality play in people’s lives, whether clients are leaning on their beliefs, questioning them, or navigating a faith transition.

In addition to her core specialties, Danielle supports clients with ADHD, depression, grief, OCD, trauma and PTSD, sexual abuse, self-harm, chronic pain and chronic illness, autism, infidelity, sex therapy, parenting, and school related challenges.

Her approach blends Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Motivational Interviewing, and Solution-Focused therapy. In practice, that means learning to make room for difficult thoughts and feelings, getting clear on what matters most, and taking small, realistic steps in that direction.
