Allora is a warm, approachable therapist who works with teens, adults, and couples, creating a space where clients feel comfortable being honest about what they're facing. She specializes in ADHD, anxiety, depression, trauma/PTSD, faith transitions, parenting, self-esteem, LGBTQ+ community and women's issues.

She's also comfortable supporting clients through a broader range of concerns, including OCD, autism, bipolar disorder, grief, infidelity, pregnancy and postpartum challenges, self-esteem and school issues, chronic pain, codependency and divorce.

Drawing from DBT, EMDR, ACT, CBT, Motivational Interviewing, and Solution-Focused approaches, Allora tailors each session to fit the person in front of her rather than a one-size-fits-all method. Whether you're navigating something specific or just feeling stuck, she's ready to help you find practical tools and a clearer path forward.
