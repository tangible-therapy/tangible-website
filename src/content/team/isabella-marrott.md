Isabella enjoys working with teens, adults, and members of the LGBTQ+ community, providing a supportive and affirming space where clients can explore challenges, discover their strengths, and move toward meaningful growth.

She specializes in treating anxiety, grief, trauma, and self-esteem, and also has experience supporting clients with ADHD, depression, faith transitions, relationship concerns, codependency, parenting challenges, school issues, and self-harm. Her approach is collaborative and individualized, integrating ACT, CBT, Motivational Interviewing, and Solution-Focused Therapy to help clients build non-judgmental place to heal, gain insight and develop skills to build a meaningful life.  
Isabella has a service dog who will be present during sessions.
