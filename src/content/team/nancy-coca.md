Nancy specializes in working with kids, teenagers, and young adults. She is calm and brings a curious, non-judgmental approach to each session. She is determined to ensure her clients feel cared for and have a safe place to explore and heal. Nancy has extensive experience navigating difficult mental health conditions as well as challenging life transitions, including anxiety, depression, ADHD, autism, minority stress, trauma, and grief. She speaks Spanish and loves working with the Hispanic community.

Her therapeutic approaches include Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Motivational Interviewing, and Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT).
