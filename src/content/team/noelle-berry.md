As an LCSW and graduate of the University of Utah, Noelle takes a warm, strength-based approach to therapy. She draws from many different modalities, including EMDR and TF-CBT, to support meaningful growth and lasting change. Noelle offers a compassionate space where clients can explore who they are and move forward with greater confidence.

Noelle’s office is welcoming to all. Many of her clients feel pressure from school, family, or their own expectations while struggling with anxiety, grief, self-doubt, or communication challenges. She helps clients gain clarity, build confidence, and create relationships that feel authentic and secure. Ages 12+
