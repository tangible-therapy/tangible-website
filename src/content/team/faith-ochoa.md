Faith is warm and compassionate. She works with adults, couples, and families, helping clients feel truly heard as they navigate what's weighing on them. She helps clients working through anxiety, couples concerns, depression, faith transitions, trauma/PTSD, spirituality and religion, and family therapy, and brings deep expertise to helping clients heal and reconnect.

She's also comfortable supporting clients through ADHD, autism, chronic illness and chronic pain, grief, LGBTQ-related concerns, codependency, infidelity, infertility, parenting, pregnancy, prenatal and postpartum challenges, school issues, self-harm, self-esteem, sex therapy, sexual abuse, and women's issues. Faith works well with individuals, couples, and families navigating major life transitions, and creates a space where every member of the family feels supported.

Faith draws from IFS, EFT, and Family Therapy Models, tailoring her work to each client's needs rather than a one-size-fits-all method. Whether you're working through a specific challenge or navigating change as a family, Faith is ready to help you create more connected, fulfilling relationships in a supportive space.
