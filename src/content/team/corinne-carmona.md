Corinne is a warm and dedicated therapist who specializes in addiction, OCD, trauma, PTSD, and substance use. She works exclusively with female clients, her office is a welcoming space for kids, teens, and adults. Corinne creates a safe, judgment-free space where clients feel heard and supported as they work toward real, lasting change. Her approach is transparent, reflective and skill-based. She helps clients gain insight while developing practical tools for growth and healing. Drawing on ACT, DBT, CBT, EMDR, ERP, Motivational Interviewing, and Solution-Focused Therapy, she tailors each session to fit the client's unique needs and goals.

Corinne is also comfortable supporting clients through anxiety, depression, eating disorders, grief, faith transitions, divorce, and self-esteem.
