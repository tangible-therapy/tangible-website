Natasha Taylor is a compassionate therapist who provides a welcoming space for teens, adults and members of the LGBTQ+ community. She specializes in supporting people navigating anxiety, ADHD, depression, grief, trauma/PTSD, self-esteem concerns, spiritual or religious questions, women’s issues, sleep or insomnia, school stress, and eating disorders. Natasha understands how these experiences intersect and works to help clients feel seen, heard, and supported at every stage of life.

Drawing from CBT, EMDR and DBT and a special certification in Dance-movement therapy, Natasha blends practical skills with deep empathy to help clients create meaningful change. Whether you’re exploring identity, coping with loss, or seeking a calmer mind and more authentic life, she partners with you to develop tools for healing and growth in a safe, non-judgmental environment.
