Grace offers a warm and compassionate space where teens, adults, and couples can feel supported and understood. She specializes in helping clients and couples navigate anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, faith transitions, and relationship challenges. Grace also supports individuals working through divorce, infidelity, spirituality, women’s issues, and the emotional changes of pregnancy and postpartum.

Using EMDR and systemic models, Grace tailors her approach to each person’s unique needs. She strives to help clients build self-compassion, confidence, and resilience as they create more authentic and fulfilling lives.
