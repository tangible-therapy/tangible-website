Morgan brings warmth and compassion to her work, helping clients navigate anxiety, OCD, grief, trauma, self-esteem, spirituality, relationship concerns, and healing from sexual abuse. She works with kids (ages 6+), teens, adults, couples, families, and the LGBTQ+ community, creating a safe and affirming space for growth.

Morgan draws from a range of evidence-based approaches, including Narrative Therapy, Internal Family Systems (IFS), EMDR, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), solution-focused therapy, family systems models, Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), and Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT). She tailors her work to each individual, supporting clients in building resilience, strengthening relationships, and moving forward with greater clarity and confidence.
