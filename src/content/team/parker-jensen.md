Parker specializes in supporting individuals navigating autism, ADHD, anxiety, and trauma through a compassionate and trauma-informed lens. He integrates EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) into his work, offering a deeply attuned and effective path toward healing. His approach is grounded in empathy and understanding, creating a space where every client feels heard, valued, and empowered to make meaningful change.

Parker’s mission is to use his compassion, clinical expertise, and genuine care to help individuals build lives that feel purposeful and connected. If you're ready to begin your journey toward greater self-understanding and healing, Parker is here to support you with insight, encouragement, and care.

His therapeutic modalities include EMDR, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Motivational Interviewing (MI), and Solution-Focused Therapy.
