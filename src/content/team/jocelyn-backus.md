Jocelyn is warm and grounded. She works with teens, adults, couples, and families, helping clients feel truly heard as they work through what's weighing on them. She helps clients working through self-esteem, trauma/PTSD and family therapy. She helps brings clients rebuild confidence and repair relationships after difficult experiences.

She's also comfortable supporting clients through ADHD, anxiety, divorce, parenting challenges, and pregnancy, prenatal, and postpartum concerns. Jocelyn works well with individuals and families navigating major life transitions, and creates a space where every member of the family feels supported.

Jocelyn uses CBT, IFS, Motivational Interviewing and Solution Focused approaches. Whether you're working through a specific challenge or navigating change as a family, Jocelyn is ready to help you create more connected, fulfilling relationships in a supportive, judgment free space.
