Micheala creates a supportive and compassionate space where clients can explore their challenges and move toward meaningful change. Micheala has a special focus on supporting clients navigating women’s issues, ADHD, anxiety, autism, depression, and self-esteem concerns. She believes that healing begins when individuals feel seen and understood, and she works collaboratively to help clients connect with their strengths and values.

To best support her clients, Micheala integrates ACT, IFS, EMDR and Solution-Focused Therapy. She tailors her approach to each individual’s needs, helping them build insight, resilience, and confidence in their ability to create the life they want.
