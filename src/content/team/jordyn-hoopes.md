Jordyn Hoopes is passionate about supporting people through life’s many challenges, whether they’re facing depression, anxiety, OCD, eating disorders, body image concerns, or other issues related to self esteem. She creates a safe and encouraging space where clients of all backgrounds can feel truly seen and understood. With a heart for helping individuals move beyond struggle and into growth, Jordyn guides each person toward greater self-acceptance and lasting confidence.

Whether addressing self-doubt, managing stress, or working toward meaningful change, Jordyn provides steady, thoughtful support.
